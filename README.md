# 👟 Adidas Sales Analysis

## 📌 Objective
Analyze US market sales efficiency across regions, retailers, products, and channels to optimize inventory and maximize profit margins.  

---

## 💼 Impact
Developed an interactive dashboard with advanced DAX measures, incorporating a dynamic "Product × Season" strategy to drive regional revenue growth. 

---

## 📊 Key Insights
Identified strong seasonal spikes; recognized Online as a high-margin, low-overhead engine for core products, while Outlet serves purely for controlled inventory clearance rather than primary profit. 

---

## 🛠️ Solution
Executed regional tactics: West early pre-orders (Q1–Q2); Northeast in-store promotions and bundling (Q3–Q4); Southeast online-exclusive flash sales (Q2–Q3); South channel correction from Outlets to Online bundles (Q3–Q4); and Midwest hybrid price testing (Q2–Q3). 

---

## 📂 Repository Structure
- `Adidas_US_Sales_Dataset.xlsx`: Raw United States sales dataset containing regional, product, and channel performance details.
- `Adidas_Analysis.ipynb`: Complete Jupyter Notebook containing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), data visualizations, and key business insights derived from the dataset.
- `README.md`: Project documentation.
